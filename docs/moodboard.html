01

Design Direction

Three principles that shift this from "template hotel site" to luxury boutique brand.

◆

Editorial Luxury

Generous whitespace, serif-forward typography, restrained color. Every element earns its place. No decoration without purpose.

◇

Asymmetric Layouts

Offset grids, left-aligned text against right-heavy imagery, varied section heights. Visual rhythm through deliberate imbalance.

◊

Cinematic Photography

Full-bleed images, dramatic crops, moody lighting. Let the real hotel photography speak. Reduce text overlays.

05

Layout Principles

Break the "center everything in a box" pattern. Varied layouts create editorial rhythm.

Left-Aligned Hero

Text anchored left with breathing room. The image does the work, the text whispers.

Asymmetric Content

55/45 splits. Alternate image-left and image-right. Creates editorial flow.

Full-Bleed Moments

Break out of the container. Edge-to-edge images and color blocks.

Varied Card Heights

Masonry-like variety feels curated, not templated.

06

Component Patterns

Minimal, restrained UI elements that feel luxury without overdesign.

Navigation

07

Motion & Animation

Animation is a privilege, not a default. Not every element animates.
